# Paper outline (working)

2. Related work: INCLUDE, iSign, WLASL; landmark-based recognition; calibration and open-set rejection.
3. ISL NOVA dataset: vocabulary selection, verification workflow, consent scopes, recording protocol, statistics (TBD).
4. Method: feature contract `isl-nova-fc-1.0.0`; models; calibration; rejection.
5. Experimental protocol: splits, leakage checks, metrics.
6. Results: official vs signer-independent split (RQ5), ablations (RQ2), open set (RQ3), conditions (RQ4). **TBD**
7. Applications: live recognition, learning, kiosk; ethical design choices.
8. Limitations and ethics.
9. Conclusion.
